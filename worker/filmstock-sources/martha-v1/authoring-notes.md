# Martha v1 — production visual source

The approved transform is the user-supplied `Martha.cube`, promoted byte-for-byte to `source/martha-v1.cube` on 2026-09-25. Its immutable source SHA-256 is `433e0af75d815254ac4837e4de15bd5055499a93493b3389f85fd4e12a1a286d`.

The CUBE was generated by DaVinci Resolve and contains 35,937 samples on a 33-point 3D grid. The package declares the Momints version 2 colour contract: SDR sRGB input and output, normalized 0–1 RGB domain, and full LUT intensity. The visual intent supplied by Momints is lush saturation, rich colors, lifted blacks, and a vintage tone.

The LUT controls colour and tone only. The app adds restrained deterministic procedural grain with `amount: 0.12`, `size: 0.30`, and `roughness: 0.34`. Exposure, halation, bloom, sharpness, and vignette remain neutral so the supplied LUT is the defining look.

The active promotional preview (`preview-02.jpg`) uses an original AI-generated view inspired by the dunes and Atlantic shoreline of South Beach/Katama on Martha's Vineyard. Its neutral, ungraded SDR sRGB source was rendered through the production Martha LUT. The initial published `preview-01.jpg` remains retained at its immutable path but is no longer referenced by the catalogue. Neither preview contains private canonical images, personal reference photographs, scans, or user captures. The private canonical corpus is used only for validation and is never copied into the package, R2, app bundle, or telemetry.

Structural analysis reports 239 interior channel components at 0 or 1 and 24 adjacent 8-bit quantization matches. Canonical comparisons therefore require explicit review of saturated colours, highlights, shadows, neutral patches, and gradients before publication. Once `martha-v1` is published, any intentional visual change requires a new versioned ID.
