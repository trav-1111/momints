# Bali 400 v1 — production visual source

The approved colour transform is the XMP-derived approximation `xmp-conversion/Kodak_Portra_400_XMP_APPROX_sRGB_33.cube`, promoted byte-for-byte to `source/bali-400-v1.cube` on 2026-09-24. It approximates the tone curve, global colour grading, and calibration-primary settings from a user-supplied Adobe Camera Raw Develop preset; it is not an Adobe-rendered transform and does not include Adobe Color's raw profile or image-dependent processing. Working and output space: SDR sRGB. The 33-point CUBE is R-fastest and uses a 0–1 input domain. `worker/scripts/generate-bali-400.mjs` validates this source and compiles the runtime atlas; it must never regenerate or replace the approved visual transform.

Creative reference: 21 privately supplied 2018 Bali scans, exposed on Kodak Portra 400 and delivered by Racquet Film. The set includes portraits, hard daylight, skies, foliage, water, interiors, and sunset. The common tendencies used here are pale warm highlights, open but slightly cool shadows, gently muted greens, and natural skin. The photos are not copied into the LUT or deployed assets.

These are unpaired positive scans. Exposure, development, inversion, and lab corrections are inseparable from the film in them. This draft is an art-directed interpretation, not a measured Portra 400 profile. It does not attempt to reproduce a single frame's haze, white balance, or exposure error.

The LUT handles tone and colour only. Grain, faint glow, mild softness, and vignette remain separate declarative recipe effects. The app applies them locally in its native Skia/GPU processing path after the LUT. Bali 400 uses deterministic monochrome procedural grain with `amount: 0.13`, `size: 0.32`, and `roughness: 0.35`; no grain texture or photograph is downloaded. The look is deliberately restrained so that the app's camera output remains usable across varied scenes.

The promotional preview is an AI-generated original digital travel scene. It was generated without scan references and its ungraded source is retained only under the non-deployed `promo-source/` authoring directory. The public preview was rendered through the approved source 33-point LUT in sRGB using `worker/scripts/render-filmstock-preview.mjs`. The preview demonstrates the LUT only; the app's grain and spatial recipe effects are not reproduced by this publishing-time preview. It is not part of the canonical test corpus.

The approved CUBE passed the private canonical digital corpus without image-level clipping or banding warnings. Structural analysis reports 2,571 interior channel components at 0 or 1, so saturated colours, shadows, and highlights require explicit visual review. Momints confirmed the supplied preset is cleared for commercial use and redistribution on 2026-09-24. The complete production recipe then passed 15 full-resolution Seeker developments with deterministic output and no processing failures, but repeated reveal runs retained excessive graphics memory. Resolve that memory issue before live deployment. No user photographs should be used as deployable previews without explicit permission. The ID may remain `bali-400-v1` while unpublished; after publication, any visual change needs a new versioned ID.
